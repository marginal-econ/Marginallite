# Administrator guide

Open `/admin` with an explicitly assigned admin account.

- Overview: platform usage, seats, waitlist, activity, and AI health.
- Users: search accounts, inspect activity, change student/admin access, reset practice data, or delete an account.
- AI providers: add, rotate, test, activate, or remove chat, embedding, and vision credentials. Stored keys are masked in the UI.
- Knowledge: upload approved syllabus, mark-scheme, examiner-report, coursebook, and past-paper material; then process embeddings.
- Calibration: maintain benchmark essays and verified marks used to hold the grading standard.
- Content: extract and review question-bank material before publishing it to students.
- MCQ papers: upload papers and mark schemes, verify answer keys and overlays, and remove retired papers.
- Diagrams: create or AI-assist Economics diagrams, preview them, and publish them into the student library.

Use a student test account after every content or access change. Treat role changes and destructive actions as audited administrative operations.
